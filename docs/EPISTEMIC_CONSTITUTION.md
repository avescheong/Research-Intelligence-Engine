# RIE Epistemic Constitution & Core Rules

> **Sovereign Epistemic Laws of the Research Intelligence Engine.**

---

## 1. Epistemic Stratification Law
Knowledge objects must never collapse into raw quotes or speculative commentary. RIE strictly separates:
1. **Evidence Objects (EO):** Verbatim, grounded factual observations directly cited from primary transcripts or empirical measurements.
2. **Interpretation Objects (IO):** Dialectical research questions, competing causal models ($H_1$ vs. $H_2$), and boundary conditions.
3. **Knowledge Objects (KO):** Highly abstract, transferable principles grounded in multiple EOs and evaluated through IO tensions.

---

## 2. Fail-Closed Human Sovereignty Invariant
1. **Zero Autonomous Canonical Promotion:** AI agents (Antigravity, Claude Code, or autonomous scripts) are strictly forbidden from setting `Status = "CANONICAL"` or `Human_Approval_State = "APPROVED"`.
2. **Candidate Review Boundary:** All synthesized objects must enter the substrate strictly with `Status = "CANDIDATE"` and `Human_Approval_State = "PENDING_LEVEL_5_REVIEW"`.
3. **Ratification Authority:** Only the human user (Level 5 Architect) may promote candidate intelligence into canonical memory, either by marking decisions inside Excel or invoking `python rie.py approve <tx_id>`.

---

## 3. Dynamic Confidence Calibration Law (GATE_SEM_KO_03)
Flat, ungrounded confidence scores (such as assigning static `0.85` across all knowledge objects) are strictly blocked by validation gates. Every Knowledge Object must dynamically compute confidence ($C \in [0.55, 0.92]$) derived from:
- Primary evidence density (EO count and verbatim span length).
- Dialectical tension resolution (presence of competing $H_2$ explanations).
- Source authority and citation clarity.
